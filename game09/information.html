Educational Information

This Basic Swedish Language Game is designed to help exchange students at Uppsala University learn Swedish in a fun way.

  • 🧩

    It lets players practice basic vocabulary and grammar through interactive activities and playful exercises.

  • 👕

    The game mainly focuses on clothing, allowing players to practice common Swedish clothing terms and the correct use of articles (en/ett).

  • 📚

    Most of the words are taken from the Basic Swedish 1 course.

  • 📈

    The game includes multiple levels of increasing difficulty, allowing players to follow the instructions and complete exercises step by step.

  • ⚡

    Players receive immediate feedback on their answers and can track their progress while playing.

  • 🔁

    After completing the game, players can review their mistakes to see what to practice next.

Have fun and learn Swedish at the same time! 🇸🇪

Created as part of the "Software Engineering and Project Management" course, Autumn Semester 2026
